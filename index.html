Before you start

What you need

A Windows PC

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, with about 400 MB free. No administrator rights and nothing else to install first.

One AI engine

The agents run on an AI tool on your PC. OpenCode is free and needs no account. Or connect your existing Claude, ChatGPT, or Grok plan.

Setup links each tool and verifies that it answers before your crew starts.

No account with us

There is no Minztobia account and no cloud middleman. What you send an agent goes directly to that AI provider, just like in its own app.

About two minutes

Install

01

Run the download

Open Minztobia-Setup.exe from your Downloads folder.

02

Choose where

It installs for your user account only. Suggested folder: AppData\Local\Programs\Minztobia. Pick shortcut and startup preferences as needed.

03

Start it

On first launch, Minztobia walks you through connecting one AI engine, and your desktop crew is ready to work.

Windows may warn you. This preview is not code-signed yet, so SmartScreen can show "Windows protected your PC". Choose More info, then Run anyway. Your browser may also ask to keep the file.
Later

Updating and removing

Updates

The app checks this site a few times a day and tells you when a newer version is available. Download the new installer and run it over the current one.

Your agents, chats, and settings are kept intact. Nothing installs silently in the background.

Uninstall

Open Windows Settings, Apps, Installed apps, Minztobia, Uninstall. It asks whether to remove your local agents and chats too; they stay unless you opt in.

Your agents and chats stay in your Windows profile, apart from the program. Removing the app leaves them unless you choose to delete them.
