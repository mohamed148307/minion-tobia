Before you start

What you need

A Windows PC

Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit, with about 350 MB free. No administrator rights and nothing else to install first.

One AI engine

The agents run on an AI tool on your PC. OpenCode is free and needs no account. Or use your own Claude, ChatGPT or Grok plan.

Setup links to each one and tests that it answers.

No account with us

There is no Minion Tobia account and no cloud. What you ask an AI goes to that AI's company, as it does in its own app.

About two minutes

Install

01

Run the download

Open MinionTobia-Setup.exe from your Downloads folder.

02

Choose where

It installs for you only. The suggested folder is AppData\Local\Programs\MinionTobia; you can pick another. Choose a desktop shortcut and whether it starts with Windows.

03

Start it

On its first start it walks you through connecting an engine, then your starter team is ready.

Windows may warn you. This preview is not code-signed yet, so Windows can show a blue "Windows protected your PC" box. Choose More info, then Run anyway. Your browser may also ask whether to keep the file.
Later

Updating and removing

Updates

The app checks this site a few times a day and tells you when a newer version is here. Come back to this page, download it and run it over the old one.

Your agents, chats and settings are kept. Nothing installs by itself.

Uninstall

Windows Settings, Apps, Installed apps, Minion Tobia, Uninstall. It asks whether to remove your agents and chats too; they are kept unless you tick the box.

Your data lives apart from the program, in AppData\Roaming\Minion Tobia.
